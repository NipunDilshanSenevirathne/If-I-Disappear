Notes

  1. AI allowance pill, live: requests left today across the free models; opens to each model's use, cooling down timers and which model does what.
  2. The boarding pass: the absence as a ticket (who, where, from when, for how long). It sets the context in one look.
  3. The headline answer: "1 of 7 things are ready". The question the product is named after, answered first.
  4. KPI tiles with change since the last update and a small trend line; each opens its page.
  5. Week-by-week chart: where the absence breaks. Hover or arrow keys show the dates and reasons.
  6. Readiness donut: the five states, each with a colour, an icon and a word (never colour alone).
  7. Every responsibility with its state, the date that explains it and the reason in plain words.
  8. Most urgent findings, linking to what we found. All charts refresh by themselves when anything changes.
  9. Filters by kind of finding: still needed while away, held by the person leaving, clashes, deadlines, not asked, sources disagree.
  10. The proof: the exact words each finding rests on, with the source they came from.
  11. False alarms set aside, and shown: the knockout check's reasons, so warnings are worth reading.
  12. Reeded glass over what only the person leaving holds, until it's handed over.
  13. Answer in place: questions the engine can't settle are asked on the card; the numbers move as soon as you answer.
