Notes

  1. Four ways in: talk, photo or PDF, type, or a short form that needs no AI at all.
  2. The recorder: a ring that follows your voice, a timer, stop or throw away; turned into 16 kHz WAV before upload.
  3. Consent first: nothing is sent to Gemini until the box is ticked, with the free tier's data terms in plain words.
  4. Every step, with the model that answered it, how long it took, and any model skipped and why.
  5. To check / accepted / everything, and accept all facts whose quotes checked out.
  6. Grouped by source, collapsible.
  7. The exact words each fact came from, and a sign that they were found in the source.
  8. Secrets: the app records that a PIN exists, never the PIN.
  9. The time you have: 30 minutes to 2 hours; the plan recomputes instantly.
  10. The result as a ring, and the minutes for each person.
  11. Mark done, saying how you know: the same typed effects the plan used, so prediction and result can be compared.
  12. Honest about what stays open, with the reason.
  13. The situation, not the steps: the person staying explains in their own words.
  14. Talk with a Gemini Live partner (or type). It sets the scene and hints, but never gives the answer.
  15. Step by step result: a key step missed means "Not yet"; a hint means "with help". A model marks, code decides.
